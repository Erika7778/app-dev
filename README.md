**The Silence of the Lambs** is about FBI agent who faces her fears while trying to catch a dangerous killer. It makes me realize that sometimes, the scariest battles are not just against other, but also within ourselves.

### Why I Like It
- Mind games
- Shows how scary the human mind can be
